A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Welcome to my
little corner of research.

Hi, I'm Qiuying! I'm a PhD student in the School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science at Queen Mary University of London.

My background is in optimization-driven scheduling and decision-making under dynamic constraints. I'm now exploring how these ideas can support more efficient resource orchestration in distributed and heterogeneous AI systems.

I'm especially interested in the intersection of intelligent optimization, collaborative AI inference, and edge–cloud computing.
